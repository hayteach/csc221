About Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Heidi!

My programming journey began in sixth grade on an Apple IIe. Since then I have worked with BASIC, Pascal, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, PHP, Perl, VBScript, Java, Python, and Flutter.

I believe that once you truly learn one programming language, learning the next becomes much easier because the underlying concepts stay the same.

My goal is to help you develop a strong foundation in problem solving and programming that will serve you throughout your career.
